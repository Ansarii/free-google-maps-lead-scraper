## 3. PhantomBuster

- **Pricing**: $69/mo to $149/mo.
- **Cost**: PhantomBuster bills by execution time (hours) rather than extracted results. Because Google Maps renders dynamic JavaScript, scraping runs consume execution minutes rapidly.

### Pros:
- Broad suite of scrapers (LinkedIn, Instagram, Google).
- Native CRM connectors.

### Cons:
- High recurring monthly subscription cost.
- Extracting emails requires chaining multiple "Phantoms" together, increasing latency and failure rates.
